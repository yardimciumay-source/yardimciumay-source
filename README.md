## 🔬 Selected Research

### 01. Germany's 2022 Minimum Wage Reform

**An Empirical Investigation of the 2022 Minimum Wage Reform in Germany: Effects on Regional Wage Inequality**

An empirical study investigating whether Germany's statutory minimum wage increase to €12 in 2022 reduced regional wage inequality across the 16 federal states.

**Research approach:**
- State-year panel data (2018–2023)
- Difference-in-Differences (DiD)
- Two-Way Fixed Effects (TWFE)
- Dynamic Event Study
- Robustness and placebo tests

**Key finding:** No statistically significant short-run effect on state-level Gini coefficients was detected within the study period.
